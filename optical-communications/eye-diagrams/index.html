Introduction

What is an eye diagram?

An eye diagram is a visual representation of a digital communication signal. It is produced by dividing a long waveform into segments, usually one or two unit intervals long, and overlaying those segments on the same time axis.

The overlapping traces form an eye-shaped opening. A wide, tall, and clearly defined eye generally indicates good signal quality. A narrow or partially closed eye indicates signal degradation caused by noise, timing jitter, limited bandwidth, dispersion, or intersymbol interference.
