Optical Transceivers

IEEE 802.3 Optical Ethernet Standards

IEEE 802.3 optical Ethernet standards define how Ethernet data travels over optical fiber, including the data rate, fiber type, wavelength, optical-lane configuration, signaling method, and transmission distance.

The names might look like alphabet soup, but they follow a logical pattern.

Understanding the Names

How to Read an Optical Transceiver Designation

A complete transceiver designation, such as SFP28 25GBASE-LR, contains two main parts:

  • SFP28 = Transceiver form factor and host-side electrical interface
  • 25GBASE-LR = IEEE 802.3 optical Ethernet interface

The first part identifies the physical module package and how it connects electrically to the host. The second part defines how Ethernet data is transmitted over the optical fiber, including the data rate, fiber type, wavelength, and reach.

Standards and Interfaces

Common optical Ethernet examples

The module names shown below are common form factors. They are defined through multi-source agreements rather than by the IEEE 802.3 interface name itself.

1G

1000BASE-SX

Common form factor: SFP

  • 1000: 1,000 Mb/s = 1 Gb/s
  • BASE: Baseband Ethernet
  • SX: Short-wavelength optical interface
  • Fiber: Multimode
  • Wavelength: Approximately 850 nm
  • Distance: Approximately 220–550 m, depending on the multimode-fiber type
10G

10GBASE-SR

Common form factor: SFP+

  • 10G: 10 Gb/s
  • BASE: Baseband Ethernet
  • SR: Short reach
  • Fiber: Multimode
  • Wavelength: Approximately 850 nm
  • Distance: Up to 300 m on OM3
25G

25GBASE-SR

Common form factor: SFP28

  • 25G: 25 Gb/s
  • BASE: Baseband Ethernet
  • SR: Short reach
  • Fiber: Multimode
  • Wavelength: Approximately 850 nm
  • Distance: Up to 70 m on OM3 or 100 m on OM4
40G

40GBASE-SR4

Common form factor: QSFP+

  • 40G: 40 Gb/s
  • SR: Short reach
  • Fiber: Multimode
  • Wavelength: Approximately 850 nm
  • 4: Four parallel optical lanes: 4 × 10 Gb/s = 40 Gb/s
  • Distance: Up to 100 m on OM3 or 150 m on OM4
50G

50GBASE-SR

Common form factor: SFP56

  • 50G: 50 Gb/s
  • SR: Short reach
  • Fiber: Multimode
  • Wavelength: Approximately 850 nm
  • Signaling: PAM4
  • Optical lanes: One 50-Gb/s lane
  • Distance: Up to 70 m on OM3 or 100 m on OM4
400G

400GBASE-FR4

Common form factors: QSFP-DD or OSFP

  • 400G: 400 Gb/s
  • FR: Approximately 2 km reach
  • Fiber: Single-mode
  • Wavelengths: Four wavelengths in the 1310-nm region
  • 4: Four wavelength lanes share one fiber per direction using CWDM
  • Lane structure: 4 × 100 Gb/s = 400 Gb/s
400G

400GBASE-DR4

Common form factors: QSFP-DD or OSFP

  • 400G: 400 Gb/s
  • DR: Approximately 500 m reach
  • Fiber: Single-mode
  • Wavelength: Approximately 1310 nm
  • 4: Four parallel fibers per direction
  • Lane structure: 4 × 100 Gb/s = 400 Gb/s
Key Comparison

400GBASE-FR4 versus 400GBASE-DR4

FR4

Four wavelengths

FR4 combines four wavelengths onto one single-mode fiber per direction using coarse wavelength-division multiplexing.

DR4

Four parallel fibers

DR4 sends four separate 100-Gb/s optical lanes over four parallel single-mode fibers per direction.
