Optical Transceivers

An optical transceiver combines a transmitter and receiver in one module. It converts electrical data into optical signals and converts received optical signals back into electrical data.

Basic Signal Flow

How a Transceiver Carries Bidirectional Data

The transmitter and receiver can operate simultaneously through separate fibers, wavelengths, or parallel optical lanes.

Transmit direction: Host electrical data → Signal conditioning and laser driver → Optical transmitter → Optical fiber

Receive direction: Optical fiber → Photodetector and transimpedance amplifier → Signal recovery → Host electrical data
