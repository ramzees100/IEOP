Optical Interfaces

Common Fiber-Optic Connectors

Fiber-optic connectors provide the physical connection between optical transceivers, fiber cables, patch panels, and test equipment. The connector type depends on the number of fibers, transceiver interface, optical standard, and application.

Back to Optical Transceivers
Connector Overview

Single-fiber and multi-fiber connectors

LC, SC, FC, and ST connectors normally terminate one fiber. MPO and MTP connectors contain multiple fibers in one rectangular connector.

LC

LC Connector

The LC is a compact single-fiber connector widely used in modern optical transceivers. Two LC connectors are commonly clipped together to create a duplex LC interface.

Typical use: SFP, SFP+, SFP28, QSFP28, QSFP-DD, and OSFP modules.

SC

SC Connector

The SC is a larger push-pull connector commonly used in telecommunications systems, patch panels, and passive optical networks.

Typical use: PON equipment, media converters, and legacy optical systems.

FC

FC Connector

The FC uses a threaded coupling mechanism that provides a stable and secure optical connection.

Typical use: Laboratory instruments, lasers, optical power meters, and precision measurement equipment.

ST

ST Connector

The ST uses a bayonet-style locking mechanism. It was common in early fiber networks but is less common in modern high-speed transceivers.

Typical use: Legacy networks, industrial systems, and older test equipment.

MPO

MPO Connector

The MPO is a rectangular multi-fiber connector that can carry several parallel transmit and receive lanes through one physical interface.

Typical use: QSFP+, QSFP28, QSFP-DD, and OSFP parallel-optics modules.

MTP

MTP Connector

MTP is an enhanced, branded implementation of the MPO connector. MTP connectors remain compatible with the appropriate MPO interface.

Typical use: High-density data-center links and parallel-optics systems.

Key Comparison

Duplex LC versus MPO/MTP

LC

Duplex LC

A duplex LC interface uses two fibers:

  • One fiber carries the transmitted signal.
  • One fiber carries the received signal.
  • Each LC connector contains one fiber.
  • Two LC connectors are clipped together.
MPO

Parallel MPO/MTP

An MPO/MTP interface carries multiple fibers:

  • Several fibers can carry transmit lanes.
  • Several fibers can carry receive lanes.
  • All fibers are held in one connector.
  • Polarity and fiber alignment are critical.

Tip

A useful connector-selection rule

SR4, DR4, and SR8 parallel-optics modules commonly use MPO/MTP connectors. LR4 and FR4 wavelength-multiplexed modules commonly use duplex LC connectors.

This is a useful general rule, but the connector must always be confirmed using the transceiver datasheet.
